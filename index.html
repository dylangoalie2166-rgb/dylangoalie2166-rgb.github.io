How it works

1

Add who owes you

Type in a client, or import your accounts-receivable report and BuckBack works out who's overdue.

2

The AI follows up

It calls in your business's name, agrees a date, texts a secure pay link and checks the promise was kept.

3

You get paid

Clients pay by card or bank transfer, straight to your bank through Stripe. You watch it all from your phone.

You stay in control

  • You set the longest a client may take to pay; the AI never agrees to more.
  • Every call, text and payment shows up in a live feed, and you're told when something needs your decision.
  • The AI always says it's an AI assistant calling from your business, calls only during permitted hours, and stops when a client asks it to.
  • A formal demand letter as a last step — only if you choose.
  • Money goes straight to your bank. BuckBack never holds it, and card and bank details are handled by Stripe.
  • Two-step verification protects every account.

What it costs

No monthly fee and no setup fee. BuckBack keeps a share of what it recovers on each account:

  • First $10,0005%
  • $10,000 to $50,0003%
  • Above $50,0001.5%

If nothing is recovered, you pay nothing. Stripe's own processing fee applies to payments made through the pay link.
