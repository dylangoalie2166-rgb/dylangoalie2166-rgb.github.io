Step 2 of 3

Before you start

Using BuckBack means agreeing to its User Agreement and Privacy Policy. These are the main points — please read the full agreement too.

  • Only add money that's really owed to your business and already overdue, with the right amount.
  • The AI contacts your clients in your business's name. It can make mistakes, and nothing it says is legal advice.
  • Demand letters go out in your name. What they say about the balance is yours to get right.
  • BuckBack keeps 5% of the first $10,000 recovered on an account, 3% up to $50,000 and 1.5% above that — also when a client BuckBack has contacted pays you directly. Record those payments.
  • BuckBack can't promise a client will pay, and its liability to you is limited.

Step 3 of 3

How BuckBack gets paid

Add a card or bank account for BuckBack's fee. You only pay when BuckBack gets you paid, and nothing is charged today.

No monthly fee: BuckBack keeps 5% of the first $10,000 it recovers on an account, 3% up to $50,000 and 1.5% above that. Payments through BuckBack's pay link have the fee taken automatically; money paid to you directly after BuckBack has been in touch goes on a monthly statement, charged to this card or bank account.
