Getting started

  1. Create your account and fill in your business details.
  2. Add a card or bank account for BuckBack's fee. Nothing is charged until BuckBack recovers money for you.
  3. Connect Stripe (Account tab → Get paid) so clients can pay you directly.
  4. Add a client, or import your AR list from the Clients tab. The AI takes it from there.

Common questions

When does the AI call my client?

At 6pm in your time zone on the day you add them (weekdays between 9:30am and 3:30pm if your clients are businesses). If they don't answer, it leaves a voicemail and tries again the next day. If they give a date they'll pay by, it calls back that day. After 5 unanswered calls in a row it stops and lets you know. It never calls on a Sunday or a BC statutory holiday.

In a hurry? Open a client and tap Call now — between 7am and 9pm Monday to Saturday, or 1pm to 5pm on Sunday.

Can I upload my whole AR list?

Yes. Tap Import on the Clients tab and upload your AR aging report (PDF, a photo, or a CSV export), then your customer list for phone numbers and emails. BuckBack works out what's actually overdue for each customer from their payment terms, leaves current balances and credits alone, and lets you review everything before the AI starts. Upload next month's report and anyone who has paid is detected automatically. For business customers, set "My clients are: Businesses" in the Account tab so the AI calls on weekdays during business hours.

What should my AR report include?

Most accounting systems (QuickBooks, Xero, Sage…) can print an AR aging report — upload it as is. If you're building one yourself, use our template: AR report template (CSV). One row per customer, all in one file:

  • Customer # and name — the name the AI will use on the call.
  • Payment terms — e.g. Net 30. BuckBack only chases what's past those terms.
  • Total owing, then how much of it is 0–29, 30–59, 60–89, 90–119, 120–149, 150–179 and 180+ days old. Use a minus sign for credits.
  • Accounts payable contact, phone and email — the phone lets the AI call; the email lets it send a demand letter.
  • Last payment date and amount — optional.

Open the template in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, replace the example rows, and save it as CSV. The examples show the three cases: Example Plumbing is collected ($3,250 is past its 30-day terms), Sample Builders isn't due yet (60-day terms), and Demo Cafe has a credit, so it's left alone.

If your accounting system's report has no phone numbers, the app asks for your customer list after the upload (contact list template) and matches it up.

What's a demand letter?

It's the last step, after calls and texts and before a tribunal or small claims court: a formal email from your business asking the client to pay within 14 days, with your payment link. It lists what has actually happened with that client — calls answered, voicemails, dates they agreed to — taken straight from their history. Each client gets one letter only, and you get a copy.

BuckBack sends one automatically if a client doesn't answer 5 calls in a row, or tells the AI to stop calling them. You can also send one yourself from the client's page. Either way, a letter only goes once you've added the client's email, what the balance is for, and its date: the invoice date if you gave them an invoice, or the date you did the work if you didn't.

When you send one yourself, the app asks you to confirm you're prepared to file a claim if it's ignored, because the letter says your business intends to. Automatic letters say that only if you've switched on "I'm prepared to file a claim" in the Account tab; otherwise they say you may. You can turn automatic letters off there too.

A letter from a sender your client hasn't heard from before can land in their junk folder. They get a text telling them to look for it (unless they've opted out of texts), and you can always forward your copy from your own email address.

Can I just ask someone what's going on?

Yes. The Assistant tab is an AI assistant that can see your account. Ask it in your own words where a client's payment stands, what the AI has done, how a feature works, or why something isn't working. It isn't a lawyer or an accountant.

You can also ask it to do things, like "record $200 from Jordan" or "call Sam now". It puts a button under its reply, and nothing happens until you tap the button and confirm, exactly as on the client's own page.

How does two-step verification work?

Every BuckBack account is protected by two-step verification: signing in takes your password and then a 6-digit code from your phone, so someone who learns your password still can't get in. You set it up once, when you create your account:

  1. Tap Add to my authenticator. Your iPhone's Passwords app opens.
  2. Choose your BuckBack login from the list. If there isn't one, tap + in Passwords and add it (website buckback.ca, your email and password), then go back to BuckBack and tap the button again.
  3. Passwords now shows a Verification Code for BuckBack: 6 digits that change every 30 seconds.
  4. Go back to BuckBack, type in the 6 digits and tap Turn on.

Next time you sign in, the code is in the same place: Passwords → BuckBack → Verification Code. Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator and 1Password work too, using the setup key shown on the same screen.

Getting a new phone? Before you wipe the old one, go to Account → Security → Move to a new phone or app. Lost your codes? Email us from your account's email address and we'll help you back in. Your card and bank details are never stored in BuckBack; Stripe holds them.

How do I tell the AI something about a client?

Use Notes for the AI: when you add a client, on the client's page, or by telling the Assistant ("Jordan works nights, evenings are best"). The AI keeps it in mind when it calls or texts that client. Notes are background only. They can't change the amount, the pay-by limit or the calling hours, the AI doesn't read them out or share them with anyone else, and they aren't used in demand letters.

Prefer to talk? Tap Call me on the Assistant tab and it phones the mobile number in your Account, so you can ask out loud. Up to 3 calls a day, 10 minutes each.

I forgot my password

On the app's sign-in screen, type your email and tap Forgot password? We'll email you a link to choose a new one. Check your junk folder if it doesn't arrive in a minute or two.

What's the weekly summary?

Every Monday morning BuckBack emails you what came in last week, what's still owed (and how overdue), who has promised to pay this week, and anyone who needs you. Turn it off any time in the Account tab.

How do I get paid?

In the app, open the Account tab and tap “Get paid” to connect Stripe. Clients pay through a secure link the AI texts them, and the money goes to your Stripe account. Clients can pay by card, or by bank transfer (pre-authorized debit) for larger balances.

What does BuckBack cost?

Nothing unless BuckBack recovers money for you. Then it's a share of what's recovered on each account: 5% of the first $10,000, 3% up to $50,000, and 1.5% above that — far below what a collection agency charges. Payments through BuckBack's link have the fee taken automatically. If a client pays you directly (cheque, EFT…) after BuckBack has been in touch, record it in the app (or it shows up on your next AR report) and the fee is billed on a monthly statement to the card or bank account you add when you sign up. There's no monthly fee and no setup fee.

Why do I need to add a payment method?

The AI only contacts your clients once a card or bank account is on file for BuckBack's fee. If a monthly statement can't be charged, the app asks you to update it, and the AI pauses if it stays unpaid for a week. If you delete your account, any fees not yet billed are charged then.

A client told the AI they already paid. What now?

The AI pauses and texts you. Check your records, then open the client in the app and tap Yes, they paid or No, they didn't. If they didn't, the AI picks up again. If you don't answer within 7 days, the payment counts as received and the fee goes on your next statement — you'll get a reminder 2 days before.

Can I stop the AI from contacting a client?

Yes. Record the payment or delete the client, and the AI stops. Clients can also text STOP or tell the AI to stop calling them. That ends the AI's calls and texts, not the balance: if the client has an email on file and automatic demand letters are on, BuckBack emails them a demand letter, and you can follow up in writing yourself.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to the Account tab → Delete account. This permanently removes your data and stops all calls and texts. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I got a call or text from BuckBack

A business you owe money to uses BuckBack to follow up. Please contact that business directly with questions about the bill — replying to a demand letter email reaches them directly. To stop the calls and texts, reply STOP to the text, or tell the assistant on the call. The balance is still owing after that, and the business may write to you about it, including a formal demand letter.
